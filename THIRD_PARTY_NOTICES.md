# Third-party references and media

Original Devast.io game, design, and artwork: LapaMauve. Original music: MonPlaisir. Reference text: Devast.io community wiki contributors; revision and attribution URLs are retained in research/source data and the asset manifest.

The repository MIT license applies to original remake code, not to third-party game artwork, sound effects, music, or reference text. Original art and effects have no established general reuse permission in the provided research. The soundtrack album links CC BY 4.0, but its downloaded archive requests permission for reuse. This conflict is unresolved. The artist's notice is preserved in the local media bundle.

The Stage 2 source and Windows builds include the selected runtime media requested for this remake. This distribution does not establish permission to reuse or redistribute third-party media. The complete Stage 1 reference collection is not included. Replaceable asset mappings, original source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are retained in `public/asset-provenance.json` (inside the installed build at `dist/asset-provenance.json`). The soundtrack archive's original notice remains in the media bundle. No creator has been contacted on the owner's behalf.

Twenty PNG replacements were generated or restored using the supplied visual references; their prompts, source roles and derivative status are recorded in `research/replacement-assets.json`. They are not represented as recovered original assets or as clearing rights in the source artwork.
